# Reviewer agent

## What it can do

Reviews a manuscript against the target venue's review criteria and returns comments.

## When to hand off

When the paper draft is ready, before the handoff package goes to the user.

## Briefing

The paper draft, the Claims list and the evaluation summary, the target venue and its reviewing criteria. It cannot see your system prompt, so pass on the red lines that matter: claims rest only on recorded evidence, and citations must be verified.

## What it returns

Reviewer-style comments: the strongest objections, unsupported claims, missing controls or baselines, and a recommendation.

## How to check

Every objection points to a specific claim or passage; address each one or record why not.
