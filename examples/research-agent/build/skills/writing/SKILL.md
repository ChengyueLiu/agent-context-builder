# Paper writing

# Paper writing

Turn the claims and evidence into a first draft that reviewers can follow and cannot find serious flaws in. This step comes after Evaluation, so every result the paper reports has already been decided by its pre-registered rule; a refuted thesis is written up as honestly as a supported one.

## Inputs

| Input | Where |
|---|---|
| What the paper may state, and how strongly | `claims.md`: each entry's status, evidence and limits |
| The verdicts | `experiments/summary.md`: numbers against thresholds, drops, what the result does not license |
| Every number | `experiments/metrics.csv` and the run directories under `experiments/runs/` |
| The thesis and the method | `method/proposal.md`, `method/method_spec.md`, `method/falsification.md` |
| Related work and citable papers | `library/landscape/landscape_report.md`, `library/corpus/CORPUS_PAPERS.csv` |
| Target venue | `brief.md` |

## Steps

1. **Outline from the Claims list.** Each section covers one or more claims. The claims Evaluation decided are the results; `open` and `contested` entries, and every `limits:` entry, feed the Limitations section.
2. **Build the bibliography from verified citations only.** Run `export_citations` over the corpus with the documents the paper draws on (`claims.md`, the landscape report, `method/proposal.md`) as `used`, writing `paper/refs.bib`, and cite with the keys it generates. An entry marked `% UNVERIFIED` lacks a DOI and an arXiv id: verify it by search before citing it, or leave it out. A paper that is not in the corpus is not citable until a search has found it.
3. **Do the figures and tables first**, and write the text around them. Take every number from `metrics.csv`.
4. **Write the text at the strength of the evidence.** State no claim more strongly than its Claims list entry supports; state a pilot result as a pilot result; carry the summary's "what this does not license" into Limitations.
5. **Check the draft** against the done-when criteria below, item by item, and fix anything that fails.

## Output

The draft under `paper/`, written in LaTeX in the language and template the target venue requires, with `paper/refs.bib`. Use vector formats for figures, with a consistent color scheme throughout the paper. The draft goes into the handoff package at Deliver.

## Done when

- Every number in the paper can be found in `experiments/metrics.csv` or a run directory.
- Every citation key is in `paper/refs.bib` as generated by `export_citations`, and no cited entry is marked UNVERIFIED.
- Every claim has a corresponding figure or table in the main text.
- No claim is stated more strongly than its Claims list entry supports.
- The Limitations section honestly states the unsupported claims (refuted, open, contested) and the known issues (drops, degradations, limits).

## Outputs

- **Paper draft** (`paper/`): The LaTeX draft in the target venue's template, with figures and tables. When to write: The Paper writing step. Write a new version, keep the old one.
- **Bibliography** (`paper/refs.bib`): BibTeX from the corpus for the papers the draft draws on; unverified entries marked. When to write: Paper writing, generated by `export_citations` before citing. Can be rewritten.
